Why counselling

Most fitness advice fails because it never met you

A generic macro sheet or a copy-pasted workout doesn't know about your knee, your night shifts, or the fact that you hate the gym on Mondays. Counselling starts with your specific life and works forward from there.

01

Trained to listen, not just prescribe

Sessions start as conversations — about sleep, stress, work travel and food history — before any programme gets written.

02

Plans that flex with your week

Missed a session or a bad food week? The plan adjusts with you instead of making you feel like you failed it.

03

Built for the long run

The goal isn't a 12-week transformation photo. It's habits that are still standing a year later.

"I don't hand out plans. I ask why the last five didn't work, and we build the sixth one around your actual life."

— Mr. Vora, Founder, mrfitnessvora
