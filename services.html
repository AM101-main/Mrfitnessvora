Services

Four ways to work with mrfitnessvora

Every service below is delivered one-on-one, online or in person. Start with a single counselling session, or step into a full programme — nothing is bundled until you're ready for it.

Most popular

1:1 Fitness Counselling

Weekly sessions covering training design, recovery, and the habits that hold both together. Best if you want ongoing support without committing to a fixed programme.

  • Weekly 45-minute session, online or in person
  • Training plan reviewed and adjusted every week
  • Direct message access between sessions
Weekly · Ongoing
Add-on

Nutrition Planning

Food guidance built from what you already eat, not a meal plan copied from someone else's kitchen. Pairs well with counselling, or stands alone.

  • Full food history and habit review
  • A flexible framework, not a rigid meal plan
  • Monthly check-in and adjustment
Monthly review
Structured

Transformation Programme

A structured 12-week arc combining training, nutrition and habit coaching — for people who want a defined start and end point with a clear target.

  • 12 weeks, training + nutrition + mindset combined
  • Weekly sessions plus a written plan each phase
  • End-of-programme review and maintenance plan
12-week programme
Single session

One-Time Consultation

A single deep-dive session for people who want direction, not ongoing supervision — a second opinion on a current plan, or a starting point to work from on your own.

  • 60-minute session, online or in person
  • Written summary and next steps after the call
  • No ongoing commitment required
One session
